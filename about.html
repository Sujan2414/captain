{ Our Journey }

Milestones in our growth

2005Where it began

It starts at the quarry

Black basalt, extracted in Vasai under an approved mining plan — benched working, controlled blasting inside fixed daytime windows, and a crushing circuit run to hold one gradation curve. Owning the face is what makes everything downstream repeatable.

2014Building on our own material

Our rock becomes C'Square sand

A Japanese vertical shaft impactor turns that basalt into cubical, consistently graded sand. Washed and screened to strip silt, clay and organic fines, tested in our own lab — engineered to meet river-sand standards without the environmental cost of dredging.

2018The end of river sand

Infrastructure built on our own material

Earthwork, base courses, drainage and structures — delivered with aggregate that never left our custody. One chain from quarry face to compacted layer means no arguments over gradation and no days lost waiting on a vendor's next load.

2021Onto the corridor

Realty that closes the loop

Residential and commercial development, plus society redevelopment that strengthens and upgrades ageing structures rather than replacing them. The concrete, the plaster and the aggregate all trace back to our own quarry.

2026Where we are now

Four divisions, one custody chain

25+ years of collective experience across a team of 50+, and 250+ clients served. What we quarry we crush; what we crush we engineer; what we engineer we build with — so the quality promised on paper is the quality that cures on site.
